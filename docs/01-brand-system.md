# 01 — Brand System

**Business:** DeckCraft Boston, an independent, customer-facing deck and fence company. Public materials never mention any sister business, franchise or shared ownership, and never reuse their reviews, photos, phone numbers or social accounts. (Enforced by a unit test that scans content.)

**Positioning:** "Deck Repair. Done Right. Built for Boston." · Secondary: "Repair. Restore. Protect. Build."
**Promise:** an honest repair-or-replace answer from photos, a written price before work starts, and one contact plus photo reports for property clients.

## Color: "Midnight & Copper" (Phase 2 spec, navy-led)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight navy | #172637 | Primary surfaces: header, hero text panel, dark bands, primary buttons |
| Navy 2 / 3 | #1D3045 / #2E4258 | Cards and borders on navy |
| Cedar / copper | #AD7047 | Rules, linework, focus rings, large accents |
| Cedar (AA fill) | #9A5E37 | Filled buttons/chips with white text (white on #AD7047 is only 4.05:1) |
| Cedar deep | #8E5631 | Small copper text on warm white (5.3:1) |
| Cedar light | #C99470 | Copper text on navy (5.8:1) |
| Warm white | #F5F2EC | Page canvas |
| Muted slate | #617080 | Secondary text (4.54:1 on warm white) |
| Charcoal | #24282D | Body text (13.3:1) |

No gradients, glow cards, emoji tiles or fake counters. Photo overlays are flat navy at 58–62% only where text sits on a photo.

## Type
Fraunces (500–700) for editorial headlines; Inter (400–700) for body/UI. Self-hosted variable fonts (no Google Fonts call). H1 56–58px desktop / 38–40px mobile, line-height ~1.05. Eyebrows: Inter 500, 12px, uppercase, 0.15em tracking.

## Wordmark
"Deck" in white (on hunter) or hunter (on cream) + "Craft" in linen, Fraunces bold, plus "BOSTON" in Inter 11px, tracked 0.25em, linen.

## Components
Buttons: Linen (primary, on hunter) · Hunter (primary, on light) · Ghost (linen border, on hunter). Minimum 48px tall, radius 4px. Cards: hunter-mid with a hunter-light border that turns linen on hover; white with a soft shadow on cream. Before/after slider: keyboard accessible (range input), Before/After pills, town + service pills, and an **"Illustrative example"** badge whenever no real photos are supplied.

## Voice
Plain, specific, a little dry. Prices as ranges with the disclaimer. Safety language is factual, never fear-mongering, and never claims an inspection or certification from photos or tools. Property copy is professional and board-ready, with no hype.

## Imagery rules
Real job photography first (architectural, natural light, New England context). Concepts are labelled. Never pass two unrelated photos off as a before/after. Photo use on the site or in ads requires `publish_permission`.
