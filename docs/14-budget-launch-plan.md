## Launch timing note
Today is early November. Staining windows are closing, so the first 90 days should **(a)** capture remaining fall sealing, **(b)** sign property-program winter walkthroughs (budget season for boards), and **(c)** fill the spring calendar.

## 30 / 60 / 90
**Days 1–30 — Foundation and soft launch**
- Owner closes the launch gates (README). Deploy. Run the docs/08 verification. GHL live with A2P approved.
- GBP verified; citations batch 1–2; GSC/Bing submitted.
- Google: Brand, Deck Repair, Stain/Seal, Property campaigns (lean). LSA application (Fencing).
- Meta: S3 Seal Before the Freeze, F1, P1 + retargeting.
- Outreach: 50 self-managed condo associations and 25 management companies (postcard + email → /request-walkthrough). Join CAI New England / MassLandlords if it fits.
- Photograph every job (before/after) with permission.

**Days 31–60 — Learn**
- Weekly search-term and creative cycles. First offline conversion imports (Estimate Booked).
- Switch Meta to Reserve-Spring creative (Dec). Launch W1–W3.
- Publish posts per the calendar; first case study; gallery reaches its first real pairs.
- Landing page vs instant form test on Meta.
- Decide on the intent-audience test (vendor due diligence done).

**Days 61–90 — Scale what works**
- Move Google bidding to Target CPA on campaigns with 30+ conversions.
- Reallocate budget to the lowest cost per booked estimate. Raise spend 20%/week where it's holding.
- Convert winter walkthroughs into spring capital-repair contracts.
- Prepare spring: F4/R-series creative, the spring announcement bar (automatic in March), and updated town pages with local jobs.
- 90-day review: CAC by channel, close rates, ACV, then set peak-season budgets.

## Weekly optimization procedure (every Monday)
1. Pull the KPIs (docs/12) by channel and campaign; compare to last week.
2. Google: the search-term routine (docs/05). Meta: the creative routine (docs/04). LSA: dispute invalid leads, check the responsiveness score.
3. Lead handling: median time to first contact, % with photos, show rate. Fix process problems before buying more traffic.
4. Budget: shift ≤20% per week toward the best cost per booked estimate.
5. Landing pages: check form-step drop-off (form_start vs generate_lead). Test one change at a time.
6. Report (docs/12) by noon ET.
